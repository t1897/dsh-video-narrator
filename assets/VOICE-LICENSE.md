# Voice reference samples

`voice_female.mp3` and `voice_male.mp3` are voice reference samples supplied by the repository owner for use with this bundle.

They are **not** covered by the repository's MIT software license. Do not use them to impersonate any person, and do not redistribute them as standalone voice assets.

If you prefer a different voice, pass your own authorized reference audio with `--voice-ref` and its transcript with `--voice-ref-text`.
